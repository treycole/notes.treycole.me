Welcome to my **Digital Garden**, a collection of ideas and exploratory writing. Unlike a traditional blog, this space thrives on spontaneity and creative exploration. I write my notes in **Obsidian** using markdown and transform them into this interactive web experience with the use of **Quartz 4**.

Here, you’ll find a collection of notes on #Physics, #Math, and #Coding and the occasional #Blog posts. I might also share updates on #Travel someday.

Explore the “knowledge network”, a dynamic connectivity map that reveals the relationships between all my notes. Simply click the button in the upper right to expand the graph.

> [!warning] Disclaimer
>  Some of these notes are from sources I have not yet cited. I will try to add citations as I go.
